## BLOCKING FIXES

1. **Expire the price independently of the edition match.** In the controlled $99.99 → $4.99 Steam sale, a fresh search at hour 23 made **zero further upstream calls** and still yielded a $95 saving on the $4.99 retailer offer. The client has a second expiry gap. An uninterrupted online tab with a 24-hour-old successful $32.99/$39.99 query still classifies it as live. Keep the package identity for 24 hours, carry a verification time for each price, and enforce the existing **five-minute price window** on both inputs to a saving. On expiry, render “Last checked …” and suppress the saving, radar win and buy alert until refresh succeeds. Add both clock-advanced cases to verification. A **$35 retailer offer versus a $25 matched Steam package** can say “cheaper on Steam” while the primary Buy action opens the $35 retailer. Derive the stat, dot, ledger and winning store from the same cent-based result. When the matched Steam package wins, link Buy to its exact `/sub/{packageId}/` page.
